Of the roughly eight billion people alive right now, one of them has been alive the longest. Since 30 April 2025, that person has been Ethel Caterham — born in Hampshire in 1909, before the First World War, before antibiotics, before most of the attempts in this archive were made. She has outlived all of them, every alchemist and every scientist, by doing nothing in particular.

She is not immortal. She is simply the current frontier: the furthest into life any living human has managed to get. This page tracks that frontier in real time.

—% of the way to the all-time record

The all-time record belongs to Jeanne Calment of France, who died in 1997 at 122 years and 164 days. No verified human has come within three years of it since. If Ethel Caterham holds, she equals the record on 1 February 2032 — and on 2 February 2032, for the first time in thirty-five years, the record moves.
